About Meet My Crew

Project Overview

Meet My Crew is a location-based digital platform designed to connect creative professionals such as actors, directors, editors, cinematographers, and content creators within the same geographical area.

The platform helps reduce production costs, improve collaboration, and promote local talent by allowing users to discover, communicate, and collaborate easily.

Objectives

  • Connect creative professionals within the same location
  • Reduce production cost and unnecessary travel
  • Improve visibility for emerging talents
  • Facilitate fast and efficient collaboration
  • Support growth of the creative industry

Target Users

  • Actors
  • Directors
  • Cinematographers
  • Editors
  • Producers
  • Content Creators
